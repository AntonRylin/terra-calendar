About

Why another calendar?

Every mainstream calendar looks the same: a wall of identical boxes. But your month isn’t identical — some days are deadlines, some are birthdays, some are the trip you’ve been waiting for all year. Terra Calendar lets your calendar show that, with colors, gradients and images that make time feel like yours.

It’s built local-first: the free desktop app keeps events in a plain file on your machine or in your own iCloud / Google account, and works fully offline. No telemetry, no lock-in — your data stays yours.

Terra Calendar is developed independently. If you like where it’s going, join the Discord or go Cloud — it all helps.
