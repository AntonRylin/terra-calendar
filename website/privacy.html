Privacy Policy

Terra Calendar · Last updated: 18 June 2026

Terra Calendar ("the app") is a desktop calendar application. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled. The short version: the app stores your data on your own device and in your own calendar accounts. The developer operates no server and does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Your calendar data. When you connect a Google account, the app uses the Google Calendar API to read and write events in the calendars you choose. When you connect Apple iCloud, it does the same over CalDAV. Calendar data flows directly between your device and your chosen account provider.
  • Your account email. Read only to display which account is connected and to label local data.
  • Authentication tokens. OAuth tokens (Google) and app-specific passwords (Apple) are stored in your operating system's secure keychain on your own device. They are never transmitted to the developer.

How your data is used

Calendar data is used solely to provide the app's functionality — displaying, creating, editing, and deleting your events on your device. It is processed locally. The developer has no servers and receives no copy of your events, tokens, or account information.

Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

Terra Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data obtained via Google APIs (your Google Calendar data) is used only to provide and improve the app's user-facing calendar features on your device, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features or as required by law, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with your consent or as required by law.

Data sharing

We do not share your data with any third party. The only external parties involved are the calendar providers you connect (Google, Apple), which handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Because the developer stores none of your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Locally cached data and stored tokens are removed when you disconnect an account in the app or uninstall it. You can also revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in your Apple ID security settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
